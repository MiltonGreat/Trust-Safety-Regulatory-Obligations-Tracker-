# Trust & Safety Regulatory Obligations Tracker

**A structured obligations register for EU and UK online platform regulations, with internal guidance and coordination artifacts.**

---

## What This Is

This repository contains a sample regulatory obligations tracker for a fictional online platform ("ConnectSphere") operating in the EU and UK. It demonstrates how a Trust & Safety Regulations Analyst would:

1. **Track regulatory obligations** from the EU Digital Services Act (DSA) and the UK Online Safety Act (OSA).
2. **Map each obligation** to a specific product feature, an owner, a status, and the evidence required for compliance.
3. **Produce internal guidance** that translates a single regulatory article into operational requirements for product, engineering, and legal teams.
4. **Coordinate implementation** through a project memo that assigns responsibilities, tracks actions, and defines approval criteria.

The goal is not to provide legal interpretation. It is to demonstrate a **repeatable methodology** for regulatory tracking and implementation coordination.

## Methodology

The tracker follows a five-step process:

1. **Identify** — Extract obligations from primary legislation (DSA, OSA).
2. **Map** — Assign each obligation to the product feature it affects, the team responsible, and the evidence required.
3. **Assess** — Determine whether the obligation is "Mapped," "In Progress," or "Not Started."
4. **Document** — Produce internal guidance that explains what the obligation requires in plain language.
5. **Coordinate** — Create an implementation memo that assigns actions, timelines, and approval gates.

This methodology is transferable to any regulatory framework, including Canadian legislation such as the proposed **Safe Social Media Act (Bill C-34)**.

## Important Caveats

- **This is a demonstration project.** ConnectSphere is fictional. The obligations listed are proposed operational mappings, not legal determinations that every provision applies.
- **Legal interpretation is required.** DSA exemptions and platform classification, and the OSA service-scope and child-access tests, must be assessed by qualified legal counsel.
- **Not legal advice.** This project is for portfolio demonstration only.
